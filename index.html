CASA TUPKA
BLANCO

The Purest Expression

Blanco tequila — also called Plata or Silver — is bottled immediately after double distillation, or rested no more than 60 days. No wood. No time. No smoke screen. Just the pure soul of Blue Agave in crystal-clear form — the most honest expression of tequila’s terroir.

PalateBright agave sweetness, zesty lime, subtle earthy pepper

AgeUnaged — Crystal Clear & Pure

EXPRESSION 02

CASA TUPKA
REPOSADO

The Rested One

Reposado means ‘rested’ in Spanish — and rest is exactly what transforms this spirit. Aged 2 to 12 months in American or French white oak barrels, Casa Tupka Reposado emerges with a golden hue and an extraordinary balance: still vibrantly agave-forward, now layered with warmth, vanilla, and complexity.

AromaWarm vanilla, toasted caramel, dried citrus peel, oak
PalateRich vanilla cream, butterscotch, cinnamon spice, agave
FinishLong, warming — oak, subtle pepper, whisper of dried fruit
Age2–12 Months in American White Oak

THE VISIONARY

The Man Behind
The Legend

Born Hirdesh Singh on March 15, 1983, in Hoshiarpur, Punjab, Yo Yo Honey Singh is formally trained at the Trinity College of Music, London — and returned to India with an unstoppable creative vision that reshaped the sound of an entire nation. At his peak, he became the highest-paid music artist in the Bollywood industry, with tracks like ‘Brown Rang’, ‘Angreji Beat’, and ‘Lungi Dance’ dominating charts worldwide.

Now, Yo Yo Honey Singh turns his legendary creative energy to the world of premium spirits. He didn’t hire someone to make Casa Tupka — he went to Mexico himself, working in the agave fields, learning the craft firsthand, bringing the same hands-on obsession to tequila that he has always brought to music.

CASA TUPKA • Blanco

UNMASKED.
UNAGED.
UNDENIABLE

Blanco tequila — also known as Plata or Silver — is the unaged soul of the spirit. Bottled immediately after the second distillation, it undergoes zero time in oak barrels. What you pour is precisely what the agave gives you: raw, honest, and brilliantly clear. In the world of tequila, Blanco is not a starting point — it is a standard. It demands the very best agave, the most skilled distillation, and zero room to hide imperfection.

"Transparency has always been my standard. In music, I never hid behind the noise. Casa Tupka Blanco is the same — nothing hidden, nothing diluted. Pure agave. Pure intention."

— Yo Yo Honey Singh

CASA TUPKA • Reposado

SOME THINGS ONLY GET BETTER WITH TIME

Reposado is the Spanish word for "rested" — and no name in tequila is more perfectly chosen. After the intensity of distillation, Reposado tequila is laid to rest inside oak barrels for a minimum of two months and up to one full year. This is the middle world of tequila — where the wild brilliance of the Blue Agave meets the gentle civilisation of the barrel. The result: extraordinary balance, warmth, complexity, and an unmistakable golden hue.

"The greatest tracks I ever made weren't rushed. They were built, layered, and left to breathe until every element was exactly right. Casa Tupka Reposado is that patience in a bottle."

— Yo Yo Honey Singh
